# Google Workspace (GWS) Suite Skill

Playbook for orchestrating and automating Google Workspace services and business productivity workflows.

## 1. Automation Recipes
- **Executive Assistant**: Parse inbox messages, categorize urgent emails, draft contextual replies, and schedule meetings.
- **Project Reporting**: Extract project metrics from Google Sheets, generate formatted summary Docs, and distribute via Gmail.
- **File Management**: Organize Google Drive folders, share permissions, and export docs to PDF.
