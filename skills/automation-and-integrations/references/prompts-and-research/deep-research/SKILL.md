# Deep Research Skill

A structured research methodology for conducting thorough investigations, market landscaping, and technology evaluations.

## 1. Research Protocol
1. **Scope & Query Matrix**: Decompose the core research question into 5–8 targeted sub-queries.
2. **Broad Survey**: Query search engines to identify authoritative primary sources and consensus views.
3. **Deep-Dive Extraction**: Extract data points, statistics, benchmark figures, and implementation details.
4. **Synthesis & Cross-Validation**: Reconcile conflicting claims, synthesize findings, and provide exact source citations.
