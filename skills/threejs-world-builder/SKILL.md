# Three.js 3D World & Environment Builder

A consolidated system for authoring rich, realistic 3D environments, outdoor natural worlds, architectural scenes, and atmospheric rendering in Three.js and WebGL.

When authoring 3D scenes or environments, locate the corresponding topic in `references/<category>/<topic>/SKILL.md`.
