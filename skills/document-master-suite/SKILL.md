# Office Documents Master Suite

A comprehensive toolkit for programmatically generating, editing, styling, and analyzing office documents across Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF files.

When creating or modifying office documents, locate the corresponding topic in `references/<format>/<tool>/SKILL.md`.

---

## Catalog & Quick Navigation

| Document Format | Subpath | Key Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **docx-master** (Word) | `office-formats/docx-master` | Creates, parses, and edits `.docx` documents with corporate typography, custom styles, headers/footers, tables, callouts, and track changes. |
| **xlsx-master** (Excel) | `office-formats/xlsx-master` | Financial models, data sheets, automated formulas (`XLOOKUP`, `INDEX/MATCH`, `SUMIFS`), conditional formatting, pivot summaries, and chart generation. |
| **pptx-creator** (PowerPoint) | `office-formats/pptx-creator` | High-impact slide presentations, custom 16:9 master layouts, executive summaries, data cards, infographics, and speaker notes. |
| **pdf-toolkit** (PDF) | `office-formats/pdf-toolkit` | Extracts text and tabular data, merges/splits pages, applies OCR to scanned pages, generates reports, and stamps watermarks. |
